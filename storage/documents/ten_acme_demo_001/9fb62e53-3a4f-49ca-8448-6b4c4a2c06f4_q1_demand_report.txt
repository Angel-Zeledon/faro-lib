Q1 2026 Demand Planning Report - ACME Corp

Executive Summary
=================
Overall demand for Q1 2026 was 8.3% above forecast. Key drivers included seasonal
promotion uplift in Colombia (Semana Santa effect) and new distribution channels in
Medellin and Cali.

SKU Performance
===============
SKU-001 (Premium Widget): Sold 12,450 units vs forecast of 11,200. WAPE=8.5%.
SKU-002 (Standard Widget): Sold 8,920 units vs forecast of 9,100. WAPE=3.2%.
SKU-003 (Economy Widget): Sold 22,100 units vs forecast of 19,800. WAPE=11.6%.

Inventory Recommendations
=========================
Based on Q1 actuals, reorder points should be revised upward by 12% for SKU-001
and 8% for SKU-003. Current safety stock levels are insufficient for SKU-001 given
the observed demand volatility (CV=0.34).

Model Performance Summary
========================
LightGBM outperformed XGBoost on 7 of 10 SKUs. Average WAPE across all models: 9.2%.
Prophet performed best on SKU-007 and SKU-009 (strong seasonal patterns).

Recommendations for Q2 2026
============================
1. Increase safety stock for high-velocity SKUs by 15%
2. Retrain models with updated promotion calendar
3. Investigate intermittent demand on SKU-005 (zero-inflated pattern)
